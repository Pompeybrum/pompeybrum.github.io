Privacy

This is a short, plain-English explanation of what happens to your information when you use this website or get in touch.

This website

This site doesn't use cookies, tracking or analytics, and there are no contact forms. The hosting provider keeps standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security, which I don't use to identify anyone.

If you email me

I use your name, email address and anything you send me only to reply to you and, if we work together, to build and look after your website. I don't share it with anyone else or add you to a mailing list. I keep our emails for as long as we're working together, and delete them on request.

If I contacted you first

I sometimes get in touch with local businesses using contact details they've made public. If you'd rather I didn't contact you again, just say so and I'll make sure I don't.

Your rights

You can ask me what information I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email jonpompey@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
