Questions

Do I need to be good with computers?

Not at all. I do the technical side. You tell me about your business, send some photos, and let me know when anything changes.

I already have a Facebook or Instagram page. Do I need a website?

Keep them, they're great for day-to-day posts. A website sits alongside them: it shows up in Google searches, it holds your prices and opening hours, and it's yours. I'll link your socials from it.

What about a web address (domain name)?

I'll help you choose and set one up, e.g. yourbusiness.co.uk, registered in your name. Domains usually cost around £10 a year, paid directly to the registrar.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Yes. If it's slow, out of date or hard to use on a phone, I can rebuild it and move it over without losing your email.
